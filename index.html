FAQ

Some common questions.

What types of videos do you edit? +
I edit YouTube videos, Instagram/TikTok reels, corporate promos, and short films — basically any project that needs strong pacing and storytelling.
What is your turnaround time? +
Most reels and shorts are delivered within 2-3 days. Long-form YouTube edits typically take 4-6 days depending on complexity.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes — every project includes revisions within the agreed scope, so we keep refining until you're happy with the final cut.
What do you need to get started? +
Your raw footage, any brand assets (logo, fonts, music preference), and a short brief on the tone/story you're going for.
Do you work on long-term projects? +
Absolutely — I work with several creators and brands on an ongoing weekly/monthly basis.
